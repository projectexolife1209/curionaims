ABOUT

AIIMS

All India Institute of Medical Sciences Raipur is a medical college and a research public university based in Raipur, Chhattisgarh. The institute operates autonomously under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, with a mission to establish a centre of excellence in medical education, training, healthcare and research imbued with scientific culture, compassion for the sick and commitment to serve the underserved.

The Institute answers to the medical needs of a majority of the population of Central India including Chhattisgarh, parts of Eastern Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh and Odisha and is spread over an area of 104 acres. AIIMS Raipur received the Kayakalp Award by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare for being one of the cleanest campuses in the country and was awarded the ERMED usage award by National Medical Library.

02 · The Dreamscape

EXPERIENCE THE MAGIC OF ORIANA

A four-day odyssey

Where applause becomes memory, talent becomes brilliance, and strangers become kindred spirits.

Oriana, the annual college fest of AIIMS Raipur, is an ethereal dreamscape where memories are woven with the threads of passion and camaraderie. It unfolds as a surreal odyssey, leaving students enraptured by experiences that linger like starlight in a midnight sky.

Within the enchanting realm of these 4 days of wonder, memories are more than mere recollections; they are the crescendos of applause after a mesmerizing performance, the echoes of impassioned debates, and the vibrant colors and rhythms that saturate the senses.

Raw talent here is transformed into polished brilliance, it is also where friendships are forged in the fires of collective endeavor. What makes Oriana truly magical is its ability to transcend time.

These memories continue to shine brightly, serving as beacons of inspiration, sparks that ignite future dreams, and a bond that unites kindred spirits who once danced together in this ephemeral dreamscape.
