01 / About

More than a fest. It's a feeling.

Oriana is where the campus stops feeling like a campus and starts feeling like an entire universe.

A celebration of everything students create when they come together: music, performance, competition, ideas, art, sport, friendships and wildly unnecessary amounts of late-night energy.

Experience

From stage lights to spontaneous campus moments, there is always something happening.

Community

Meet people, build teams, make memories and leave with stories.
